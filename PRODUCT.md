# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students in Class 6–12 and JEE/NEET aspirants (mostly teens), on phone and laptop, studying alone and with friends. Admins (the operator) import and manage the video library.

## Product Purpose
Open Learning ("Learn anything. Learn together. Grow together.") is a free platform: a structured video library (category > class > subject > chapter > topic), self-learning pages, and Learn With Friends (discussion, chat, voice groups, friends by Student ID). Success: a student finds the right lesson fast and studies with others.

## Positioning
Free, curated, structured library driven by an admin-managed Google Sheet, combined with category-scoped study groups and voice rooms identified by permanent Student IDs (OL-YYYY-NNNNNN).

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js 16, Tailwind 4, Supabase, LiveKit. Live categories: School, JEE, NEET. Languages and Programming & AI are Coming Soon and not enterable. Question Papers, Tests and Games are designed placeholders. Student ID and role are immutable for students.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Open Learning". Tagline above. User explicitly rejects: violet/pink gradients, glowing effects, the same card pattern everywhere, generic AI-template look. Wants a professional, high-level, consistent result in every surface (public and in-portal).

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts or partner logos exist; none may be invented. Real video data comes from the user's Google Sheet.

## Product Principles
1. Findability over decoration: the library is the product.
2. Honest states: Coming Soon is labelled, never faked.
3. One calm system across public and in-portal surfaces.
4. Study-first: nothing competes with the lesson.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard use, reduced motion respected, usable on low-end phones.
